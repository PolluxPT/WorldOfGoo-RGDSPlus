World of Goo RGDS+ v1.0 video bridge

zig cc -target aarch64-linux-gnu.2.28 -shared -fPIC -O2 native_video.c -ldl -o librgds_video.so

The verified GOG build is prepared locally for direct rendering. Native SDL
targets stock display 0 and GLES. Large texture uploads use reduced-resolution
copies to lower memory use. name_overlay.c draws the controller keyboard.
The launcher powers off the lower backlight after a swap on display 0.

The renderer and D-pad/A controls were checked with the actual GOG game using
Mesa GLES. The top-display gate was exercised using simulated SDL outputs.
Actual firmware display power and speaker playback were not available to test.
